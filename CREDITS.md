The original provider attribution, source pages and direct URLs are also retained in `public/images/objects/sources.json`. The app's settings dialog links to the provider and license.

## Learning content

Example vocabulary, translations and sentences were written for this prototype. They are samples, not the result of image recognition.

The sample culture notes reference the National Institute for International Education / Study in Korea [Guidebook, essential Korean appendix](https://cdn.studyinkorea.go.kr/public/res/guide/guideBook_ko.pdf). The app uses short practical expressions and its own explanatory wording. The notes qualify their scope and do not infer institution-specific policies from photographs.

## Generated photograph

The coffee vocabulary card uses a locally bundled image made with the built-in image generation tool: `public/images/generated/coffee.png`. The prompt and original output location are retained in `public/images/generated/README.md`. This asset has no external photograph attribution. Other sample images retain the sources above.
